Common questions

How do I invite a family member?

Invitations need the Family plan, which includes 4 members. Each extra member is an add-on of $2 a month, paid by the person who subscribes. A parent in the household opens the household area of the app and chooses to invite someone. The invited person does not pay separately and does not need their own subscription.

A child added as a profile, without a phone, does not count toward the 4.

If the person already pays for their own Solo plan, they can cancel it in their Apple account settings after joining.

How does a child join with a code?

A child with their own iPhone does not need an email address. A parent creates an invite code on their phone. On the child’s phone, install Steady, choose to join with a code, and enter or scan the parent’s code. The child then sees the simpler version of the app made for children.

For a child without a phone, a parent can add them as a profile instead, so their birthday and medical records have a place.

How do I change the language?

Steady is available in English and Arabic. Language is a setting for each person, so everyone in a household can use the one they prefer. Change it in the app’s Settings. In Arabic, the whole layout reads from right to left.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, so you cancel them in your iPhone settings:

  1. Open the Settings app.
  2. Tap your name, then Subscriptions.
  3. Tap Steady, then Cancel Subscription.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current period ends to avoid the next charge. When a subscription ends, your household becomes read-only and your data is kept. For refunds, Apple decides; you can ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?

In the app, go to Settings and choose to delete your account. You can also email us and ask us to delete it.

Deleting your account deletes your private data, such as your medical record and cycle tracking. Shared household items you created, like grocery items, stay with the household. Deleting your account does not cancel your Apple subscription, so cancel that as well.

How do I turn off location sharing?

Open the iPhone Settings app, then Privacy & Security, then Location Services, then Steady, and choose Never. Steady then stops collecting your location.

Inside the app, a child cannot turn off their own location sharing. That choice belongs to the child’s parents.

Who can see my medical record or cycle tracking?

Only you, and the people you choose to share them with. Parents can see and edit their children’s medical records. Read more in the privacy policy.

Is there a free version, or an Android version?

There is no free tier, but both plans start with a 7-day free trial. Steady is for iPhone only for now.
