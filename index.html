01 · What it does

The things a household runs on, in one place.

Each part can be switched on or off, so the app holds what your home needs and nothing else.

  • Kitchen

    A grocery list everyone shares, and a pantry that knows what is at home.

    Add to the list from anywhere. Check things off in the shop. Keep track of what is in the pantry, the fridge or the freezer, so nobody buys a third jar of the same thing.

  • Reminders and countdowns

    The dates that matter, held for you.

    Set reminders for yourself or for anyone in the household. Count down to the days you are waiting for, and keep birthdays where you will see them.

  • Medical

    Test results, for each person.

    Keep each value with its normal range, so the next visit to the doctor starts with the facts.

  • Home information

    The facts of the house, kept in folders.

    The Wi‑Fi password, the meter number, the insurance policy. Store what you choose, and decide who in the household can see each part.

  • Family map

    Where everyone is, on one map.

    See family members on a shared map. Parents can see where their children are. Part of the Family plan.

  • Cycle

    A private cycle tracker.

    Off until you turn it on. Seen only by you, and by the people you choose to share it with.

    Private by default

  • Birthdays

    Relatives and the days to remember.

    Shown alongside your countdowns.

Still being designed

  • For the couple. A shared space for two people, being shaped now.
  • Workouts. A simple way to log exercise, also being shaped now.

02 · Who it is for

For one, two, or the whole family.

Steady works the same way for a person living alone and for a house full of people. Only the size of the household changes.

Solo

One person

Your lists, records and reminders, in order and in one place. If you later move to Family, everything you have logged comes with you.

Family

4 members on one subscription, with room to add more

One person subscribes and invites the others. Invited members do not pay separately. A child added as a profile, without a phone, does not count toward the 4. A couple is a household of two parents.

Parents run the household.

They invite and remove members, manage the plan, and decide what children can see.

Children get a simpler app.

A child sees a limited version made for them. A child with a phone can join with a code from a parent, with no email address.

Little ones still count.

A parent can add a child who has no phone as a profile, so their birthday and medical records have a home too.

03 · Privacy

Private means private.

A household shares a lot. Some things belong to one person. Steady is built around that difference.

  1. Private items stay private.

    A cycle log or a medical record is seen only by its owner and the people they choose. Parents can see and edit their children’s medical records.

  2. Children see a limited app.

    Parents decide what children can see. The rules are enforced in the database, not only hidden on the screen.

  3. No ads.

    Steady is paid for by subscription. There is no advertising in the app.

  4. Your data is not sold.

    Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone. Steady launches with no third-party analytics or tracking.
